# Offline Mana supplier source review

This packet reviews suppliers of the five native Mana adjustment channels
`3357`–`335b` for the unchanged supplied Original05. It grants no native
coverage, installs no zero producer, closes no import obligation and does not
make final Mana or the complete build available. It is test/authoring evidence;
production evaluation never loads these archives.

The [selected radius review](radius-review.md) additionally reconciles empty
passive-jewel construction using complete bodies and explicitly observed
invocation/slot inputs. The subsequent ordinary-passive review below covers
finite parsed-record transport; generated-skill and later-delivery obligations
remain separate.

The required source census includes Mana conversion to Energy Shield, Armour
and Evasion; direct `ExtraMana` and `ManaTotal`; and both `ConvertToMana` and
`GainAsMana` from Armour, Evasion, Energy Shield, Life and Ward. A zero-valued or
inactive source still requires review. In particular, the resource pass's
procedural zero `Conversion` records are real writes, not absence evidence.

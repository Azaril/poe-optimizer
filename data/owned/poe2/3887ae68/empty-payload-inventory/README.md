# Empty authored payload inventory

This opt-in normalization policy proves an empty inventory of relationships between saved skill occurrences. It does not calculate a trigger, generate a skill, choose an action, or grant mechanics coverage.

The policy is bound to the exact source snapshot and owned role catalog. Its Gem domain is authored from the complete pinned catalog, inspecting every constructed granted effect. Meta, trigger, triggered, inbuilt-trigger, and trigger-adding effects are excluded conservatively. `Triggerable` alone does not imply an authored container. Item-provider provenance also does not imply a relationship between saved skill occurrences. Mutable source display names, global-effect flags, and item-provider flags remain in per-build reference observations rather than the shared classifier. The saved-group proof also requires reviewed source framing, exact selectors, and at most one non-support row per group. Disabled containers still prevent an empty proof.

Declared secondary effects and stat sets remain visible in the source records, including references the pinned loader does not resolve or include in its constructed effect list. This policy does not resolve those missing definitions. Internal effects of one Gem do not create additional saved skill rows. The separately reviewed, nonphysical `EnemyExplode` source row also does not manufacture a physical Gem or an authored containment relationship.

The family allocates no definitions and changes no Core or Engine code. Publication changes only the optional normalization policy, the tree's full normalization binding, and release provenance.

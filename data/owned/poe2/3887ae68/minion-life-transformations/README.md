# Original minion Life transformation evidence

This offline packet retains the exact original resource pass for the Skeletal
Sniper in Original05 and six related controls. It publishes no native rule,
definition, default, coverage closure or final metric. The profile name selects
an oracle fixture only; it is not a production dispatch key.

The optional observer reuses the existing complete-build acquisition, with a
separate transformation flag. Original/repeat/warm controls, three passive-removal

complete loads per configured JIT mode. Instrumented observation disables JIT;
independent uninstrumented controls use their configured off/on mode. Full final
snapshots and complete cross-mode reports must agree. No business method, source
record, resource table or calculated value is replaced.

## What is established

Eight executed Actor/mode contexts retain the original resource function's exact
Actor/store/parent/summoner identities. Six unexecuted CALCS contexts have no
resource call; they are not represented as an observed neutral calculation.

- At `CalcDefence.lua:1375`, the initialized six-resource list is captured before
  any transfer. Life's `totalBase` comes from the original query at line 1372.
  Both its raw and eligible pre-transform LifeTotal inventories are empty.
- All ten incoming channels from Armour, Evasion, Energy Shield, Mana and Ward
  are retained separately, including their raw inventories, eligible records
  and supplemental sums. These domains are empty in the captured cases.
- At lines 1386/1412, each of the five original donor branches exposes its exact
  source/target objects, capped conversion rate, gain rate and combined rate.
  All are zero, and none enters its positive transfer branch.
- At line 1430, after the whole pass and before emission, Life's `globalBase`
  and `totalBase` are still zero. The observer copies these original fields; it
  does not calculate them.
- The original `NewMod` and `AddMod` calls emit ExtraLife and LifeTotal records
  at lines 1440/1441. All sixteen zero records retain their exact inserted-object
  identities. The first two Life calls precede those records; the third call at
  line 1631 reads the same records. All twenty-four original Life calls remain
  in the complete report.

The source code and these observations establish the bounded neutral case:
Life's generated inputs start at zero, and none of the five incoming branches
changes them. This does not establish positive transfers, obtainable modifier
combinations or completeness of untranslated native suppliers. In particular,
preexisting LifeTotal and the later generated LifeTotal record remain separate;
PoB appends the latter rather than replacing the former.

## Exact arithmetic

The source helper `floor(multiplier,4)` is `floor(multiplier *10000 +0.0001) /10000`; it is not an unbiased four-decimal floor. Source `round(x,0)` is `floor(x +0.5)`, including its floating-point boundary behavior. The graph spells out these operations rather than substituting the engine's mathematically robust nearest-ties-positive operation.

After adding ExtraSpirit and multiplying by the rounded multiplier, the positive branch requires MORE>0, INC>-100 and a nonzero base. It preserves source order: multiply by `(100+INC)`, divide by100, multiply MORE, divide by `(1+max(efficiency,-100)/100)`, divide efficiencyMORE, round, then clamp to zero. The inactive arithmetic branch is a lazy zero and does not demand its unused divisors. Finally it multiplies the per-use amount by `max(requested_count-free_count,0)`. Division by zero or nonfinite results are explicit numerical failures, never inferred finite source outputs.

`per_use_flat` (`3274`) and `counted_flat` (`3275`) are **arithmetic amounts**. A native amount of zero does not assert that PoB emitted its optional per-skill `SpiritReservedBase` field. This packet does not compute percentage reservation, actor totals, maximum Spirit, reserved clamping, unreserved Spirit, minion populations or FullDPS. Existing Spirit resource unit `0004`, factor unit `0001`, percentage unit `0002` and count unit `295a` are reused. Existing player Spirit metric `313c` and base resource contributor `3166` are not relabeled as reservation outputs.

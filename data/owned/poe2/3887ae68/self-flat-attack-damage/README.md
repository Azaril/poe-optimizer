# Draft minion Attack flat damage collection

The source census and the native draft have different scopes. `source-evidence.json`
and `source-review.md` inventory source behavior; they grant no native coverage.
The other JSON files define a bounded, unpublished native family using existing
owned rules, checked contribution queries, receivers and Action routes.

The two modifier definitions preserve the actual source-declared ranges:

| Source declaration | Minimum endpoint | Maximum endpoint |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `UniqueAddedPhysicalToMinionAttacks1` | 5–8 | 10–12 |
| `AddedPhysicalToMinionAttacksUnique__1` | 5–8 | 12–16 |

Each exact item-modifier occurrence reads its two required roll slots and
contributes to Player channels `3373`/`3374`, whose meaning is flat Physical
damage granted to minion **Attacks**. Ordered checked queries retain source
identity, equipment slot rank and semantic modifier position. Two receivers
bind only the existing Sniper Actor slot `001f`, producing Attack subtotals
`3375`/`3376`. The two variants share these channels; no item-specific execution
or new public transport contract is introduced.

The data's Basic `0021`/output `0022` routes are applicable to its proved Attack
selection only. They remain Partial: the Attack subtotal is not a universal
self-flat total, and other local/inherited flat sources have not been completed.
The checked source memberships also remain Partial, even when currently empty.
No canonical release, allocator, complete-build result or production input is
changed. Draft IDs `3373`–`337c` are not published allocations.

The native tests explicitly admit these modifiers on one synthetic item/template
carrier and close the two controlled query groups in that finite world. Only
there is the Attack subtotal sufficient for the physical consumer's `3371`/`3372`
self-flat ports. The tests retain the real intrinsic endpoints and combined
factor and perform no scalar injection at those ports. No legal item/template
association, obtainability, affix coexistence or discrete-roll legality is proved.
Quantity ranges preserve the existing typed item-roll representation; fractional
parser acceptance is not a game-legality claim. Missing values and out-of-range
rolls remain admission errors.

The query's cross-equipment slot ranks are authored finite-fixture order, not a
proof of PoB equipment insertion order. The witness and native order controls
cover one ordered modifier stream on one carrier. Item catalyst, quality and
other raw-to-effective roll transformations are also unproved for this family;
the finite carrier assumes none. Both limits remain explicit in the bindings
and prevent promoting these draft rules or memberships to production coverage.

review. Native subtotal comparisons use original inherited `Sum` results for
